# Essential Biodiversity Variables (EBVs)
*Last updated: 11.12.2025*

## List of EBVs relevant to ecosystems

* **Ecosystem function**
    * Primary productivity -> NPP derived from MODIS [MOD17A] or VIIRS [VNP17A]
    * Ecosystem phenology -> [annual] phenological patterns derived from Landsat, MODIS [MOD09Q1P_NDVI], or VIIRS [VNP22Q2]
    * Ecosystem disturbances -> [annual] disturbances derived from LandTrendr or CCDC
* **Ecosystem structure**
    * Live cover fraction -> fractional cover of [live] green vegetation, derived from spectral mixture analysis of Landsat or MODIS
    * Ecosystem distribution -> land cover types (IGBP classification) derived from MODIS [MCD12Q1] or VIIRS [?]
    * Ecosystem vertical profile -> heights derived from GEDI or ICESat-2, or PAI derived from GEDI, or LAI derived from MODIS [MOD15A2] or VIIRS [VNP15A2]
