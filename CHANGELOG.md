**About**: This file is intended to provide updates related to the main items presented in the Geo AI Compendium's [Readme](https://github.com/BzGEO/Geo_AI_compendium/blob/main/README.md), which contains an extensive collection of links to different GeoAI / GeoML resources.

## Updates: 06.12.2025
1. In the GeoAI / ML space, one of the *regularly updated* sources of information is the Satellite Image Deep Learning series by [Dr. Robin Cole](https://github.com/robmarkcole). His [video podcast playlist](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBnx7aJQUZE&list=PLHifi5Wnkifd5ZndO2ichD_7nnB26eDBj) was updated this week with a talk from [Julia Wąsala](https://github.com/JuliaWasala) about the detection of methane plumes using TROPOMI data.

## Updates: 03.12.2025
1. The GIS Association of Alabama (GISAA) is hosting a Friday Lunch & Learn webinar on the GeoAI Working Group (formerly under SERVIR), by [Tim Mayer](https://github.com/MayerT1) on 05.12.2025. See: https://gisaa.org/event-6388124. The recording of this will end up on the GISAA's YouTube channel (https://www.youtube.com/@gisalabama/videos).

## Updates: 14.11.2025
1. Version 3.6 of [ArcGIS Pro](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-pro/overview) was released yesterday (13.11.2025). The following article details the overall updates: https://pro.arcgis.com/en/pro-app/latest/get-started/whats-new-in-arcgis-pro.htm. The following also details the specific updates related to *Image Analyst*, including new features related to revising training data for classifications, and extraction of spectral signatures, including a new format for spectral signature data: https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/arcgis-pro/imagery/whats-new-in-imagery-in-arcgis-pro-november-2025.

Note: It remains to be seen whether or not the new version of ArcGIS and the associated new version of the [Deep Learning tools](https://github.com/Esri/deep-learning-frameworks) (see: https://github.com/Esri/deep-learning-frameworks) will result in any tangible improvements in the time taken to perform deep learning tasks.
